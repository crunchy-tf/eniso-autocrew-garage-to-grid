# Autocrew: Garage to Grid — all 75 frames

Website: https://autocrew-garage-to-grid-20261012.ayoubdhaouadi08.chatgpt.site

The core has 72 fixed frames across 37 teaching topics and takes 150 minutes. Frames 73–75 are the optional 30-minute real lab. Topic times are shared across their concept/practice/question frames; they are not repeated for each frame.

Dark and light modes are available. Pages stay within the viewport. Concepts and guided practice have separate frames; each quiz question has its own frame. Desktop labs place guidance on the left and files, the car, and terminal on the right. Long output, files, history and diffs use explicit page controls. Phones use Explanation/Illustration and Files/Car/Terminal tabs.
